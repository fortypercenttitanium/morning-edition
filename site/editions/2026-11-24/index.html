Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after days of turmoil

The board reversed course Monday, restoring the chipmaker’s co-founder to the top job after a jolt that rattled markets, AI companies and Washington.

Nvidia’s board reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive on Monday, only days after rejecting his return, in a reversal that underscored how central the company has become to financial markets and the AI industry. The Associated Press reported that shares surged after the decision, though the stock remained below last month’s peak. AP also reported that the White House viewed Huang’s return as important enough that Vice President JD Vance helped press the case with board members.

The episode lands in a market already unsettled by a selloff in AI-related stocks and by Washington’s increasingly direct involvement in semiconductor policy. Nvidia’s reach extends from cloud providers to model developers and data-center builders, so the leadership upheaval quickly became a broader question about industrial concentration, national policy and the stability of the AI boom.
