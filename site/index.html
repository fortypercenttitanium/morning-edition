Trump, Xi and Putin hold rare trilateral summit in Shenzhen

The meeting capped a whirlwind Asia trip and added a new diplomatic image to an already unsettled global order.

President Donald Trump met Thursday in Shenzhen with Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin in an unprecedented three-way summit on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation gathering. AP reported that the session produced striking symbolism but limited immediate substance, with the three governments emphasizing dialogue on major-power relations and international conflicts rather than announcing a detailed agreement.

The meeting came as U.S. allies in Europe sought reassurance about parallel Trump administration talks over ending Russia’s war in Ukraine. AP reported that Jared Kushner and envoy Steve Witkoff were in Berlin to brief nearly 40 European leaders, while Russian officials continued to frame any broader peace arrangement on Moscow’s terms.

The trilateral followed several days of competing signals from Washington, Beijing and Moscow about trade, technology and security. Even before Trump returned to Washington, analysts were treating the summit less as a settled breakthrough than as a marker of how openly the three nuclear powers are testing new formats for dealing with one another.

Sources: Associated Press · 2026-11-19

U.N. court finds Myanmar responsible for genocide against the Rohingya

The decision is the first time the World Court has found a state responsible for genocide.

The International Court of Justice ruled that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya during the military’s 2017 campaign, a landmark judgment that AP said is the first of its kind against a state under the Genocide Convention. Rights groups called for fresh military and economic sanctions, while the court’s practical enforcement powers remain limited.

Myanmar’s government rejected the judgment Friday as unfair, while the opposition National Unity Government said it respected the court’s authority. In refugee camps in Bangladesh, Rohingya families celebrated the ruling but also said that justice alone would not resolve their statelessness and displacement.

Update Flyers erase a three-goal third-period hole and stun Ottawa in overtime, 6-5
